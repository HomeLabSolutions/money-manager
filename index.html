Delete your Money Manager account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Money Manager account and the personal data associated with it.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Money Manager app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Transactions
  • Budgets
  • Categories
  • Cloud backups
  • Other personal data associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when required by applicable laws or for security purposes, including:

  • Server logs for up to 30 days.
  • Records required for legal obligations.

Need help?

If you are unable to access your account, please contact the developer and we will assist you with deleting your account and associated data.
